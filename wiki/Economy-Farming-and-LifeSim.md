### Faction Reputation & Store Discounts:
The game tracks your reputation across settlements. By completing quests and clearing surrounding mob threats, Rin unlocks tier-based store discounts:
- **Neutral (Tier 0)**: Base store pricing.
- **Friendly (Tier 1)**: 10% discount on all items & runes.
- **Honored (Tier 2)**: 20% discount + exclusive blueprint access.
- **Champion (Tier 3)**: 30% discount + Kael will repair gear for free!

## 🌾 2. Farming & Climate Mutation Strains

Farming in EchoBound is not a static grid chore; it's a botanical experiment where soil temperature and surrounding elements cause **environmental mutations**:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    CROP STRAIN GUIDE                                    |
+---------------+-------------------+--------------------+--------------------------------+
| Base Seed     | Growth Time (mins)| Climate Condition  | Resulting Mutated Crop         |
+---------------+-------------------+--------------------+--------------------------------+
| Wheat Seed    | 4.0 mins          | Standard Soil      | Golden Solar Wheat (Stamina)   |
| Sweet Berry   | 3.0 mins          | Near Water Channel | Sugar Dew Berries (Speed)      |
| Ember Seed    | 6.0 mins          | Near Lava / Arid   | Flameflower (Fire Resistance)  |
| Tide Seed     | 6.0 mins          | Snow / Sub-Zero    | Frost Orchid (Mana Regen)      |
| Void Spore    | 8.5 mins          | Dark Caves / Night | Abyssal Truffle (Invisibility) |
+---------------+-------------------+--------------------+--------------------------------+
```

### Advanced Irrigation & Care:
- **Hydration Mechanics**: Farmland within 4 blocks of water maintains optimal hydration, cutting growth time by 25%.
- **Bloom Spark Accelerator**: Casting Bloom magic over tilled soil instantly advances crop growth stage by 1 cycle.

## 🛠️ 4. The 8 Crafting Stations

Crafting in EchoBound scales as your workshop expands. Press `[C]` anywhere to craft basic survival essentials, or interact with specialized workbenches for advanced tiers:

1. **Campfire**: Basic torch crafting, simple meat roasting, and campfire spawn-point warmth.
2. **Workbench**: Wooden tools, building blocks, doors, chests, and ladders.
3. **Forge**: Smelts raw ore (Copper, Iron, Titanium) into ingots; forges metal armor plates and weapons.
4. **Loom**: Weaves plant fibers, wool, and spider silk into textiles, backpacks, and aerodynamic scarves.
5. **Alchemy Table**: Distills herbs and monster essences into recovery potions and tactical phials.
6. **Cooking Hearth**: Gourmet multi-ingredient recipes with timed stat buffs.
7. **Rune Table**: Sockets elemental runes into weapons, dynamically re-naming and enchanting gear.
8. **Ancient Forge (Lost Tech)**: Located only in deep subterranean ruins. Used to synthesize legendary artifacts from Echo Core fragments.

## 🧪 5. Alchemy & Tactical Phials

Don't go into the abyss empty-handed. Keep your quickslots stocked with alchemical brews:
- **Greater Health Phial**: Restores 6 hearts instantly.
- **Spark Surge Tonic**: Completely fills Rin's Spark Energy gauge and grants 5 seconds of unlimited dashing.
- **Dowsing Elixir**: Causes subterranean treasure chests within 30 blocks to glow brightly through solid rock.
- **Stonefoot Draft**: Grants complete immunity to knockback and stun for 12 seconds.
