## 🍇 1. The 8 Elemental Sparks (The Devil Fruits of Aethelgard)

In EchoBound, magic isn't some generic mana bar system. You wield the **8 Elemental Sparks** — fragments of the primordial frequency that grant wielders God-tier elemental manipulation, exactly like Devil Fruits in the One Piece universe:

```
[ EMBER ]     [ TIDE ]      [ GALE ]      [ TERRA ]
 (Mera Mera)   (Fish-Man)    (Goro/Wind)   (Ishi Ishi)

[ VOLT ]      [ BLOOM ]     [ VOID ]      [ ECHO ]
 (Goro Goro)   (Mori Mori)   (Yami Yami)   (Gura Gura / Sound)
```

### Elemental Breakdown:

| Spark Element | Fruit Equivalent | Primary Effect | Combat Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ember (Fire)** | *Mera Mera no Mi* | Incinerate & Burn DoT | High continuous damage; lights dark caves; sets grass ablaze |
| **Tide (Water/Ice)** | *Hie Hie no Mi* | Slow & Freeze | Freezes water into ice bridges; slows mob advance by 45% |
| **Gale (Wind)** | *Moku Moku no Mi* | Velocity & Lift | Triple jump boost; knockback gusts; deflects incoming projectiles |
| **Terra (Earth)** | *Ishi Ishi no Mi* | Armor & Density | Grants temporary hyper-armor; erects instant voxel cover walls |
| **Volt (Lightning)** | *Goro Goro no Mi* | Chain Lightning Stun | Chains between up to 4 nearby mobs; stuns robotic automatons |
| **Bloom (Life/Nature)**| *Mori Mori no Mi*| Health Leech & Regrowth| Heals player on hit; accelerates crop growth by 200% |
| **Void (Gravity/Dark)**| *Yami Yami no Mi* | Black Hole Pull & Pierce| Ignores 50% enemy defense; collapses projectiles into a vortex |
| **Echo (Sound/Vibration)**| *Gura Gura no Mi* | Shockwave & Resonance | Shatters stone blocks instantly; breaks Conductor energy barriers |

## 🌪️ 2. The 6 Signature Dual Fusions (Awakened Combos)

What happens when you combine two elemental sparks at the same time? You get an **Awakened Dual Fusion**. These are the ultimate techniques in Rin's arsenal:

### 1. Fire Tornado (Ember + Gale)
* **Visual FX**: Swirling vortex of orange-gold embers and cyclone wind particles.
* **Effect**: Pulls in all enemies within an 8-block radius while dealing 18 Fire damage per second for 4.5 seconds.
* **One Piece Reference**: Ace's *Dai Enkai: Entei* mixed with Luffy's *Gomu Gomu no Storm*.

### 2. Storm Burst (Tide + Volt)
* **Visual FX**: Blinding flash of cyan electric sparks surging through rushing water torrents.
* **Effect**: Deals 45 burst damage. If target is in water or wet biome, damage is doubled ($90\text{ DMG}$) and inflicts 3-second Paralysis.
* **One Piece Reference**: Nami's *Thunderbolt Tempo* on steroids.

### 3. Thorn Fortress (Terra + Bloom)
* **Visual FX**: Massive spiked briars and stone pillars bursting from the earth.
* **Effect**: Encircles Rin in an impenetrable barrier for 6 seconds. Any enemy that touches the barrier takes 25 puncture damage and is knocked back 5 blocks.

### 4. Phantom Copy (Void + Echo)
* **Visual FX**: Dark shadow silhouette mirroring Rin's movements with harmonic audio trails.
* **Effect**: Spawns an ethereal decoy that draws all mob aggro for 5 seconds while amplifying Rin's stealth backstab damage by 300%.

### 5. Magma Hammer (Ember + Terra)
* **Visual FX**: Colossal hammer of molten rock crashing down, fracturing the ground into lava fissures.
* **Effect**: 70 single-target blunt damage, creates 4 persistent lava tiles that deal fire damage to anything walking on them.

### 6. Blizzard Dash (Tide + Gale)
* **Visual FX**: High-speed icy jet leaving a trail of razor frost shards.
* **Effect**: Rin dashes forward 12 blocks instantly, freezing all enemies passed through for 2 seconds.

## ⚔️ 3. 3-Socket Runecrafting (Advanced Armament Haki)

You know how in One Piece, top-tier fighters infuse their swords with **Ryuo (Advanced Armament Haki)** to turn a regular blade into a Black Blade? That is literally **Runecrafting** in EchoBound.

At Kael's **Rune Table**, any forged weapon (Sword, Staff, Hammer, Bow) can be upgraded with up to **3 elemental runes**:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       RUNE SOCKET ARCHITECTURE                        |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
| Rune Type         | Stat Bonus / Socket| Visual Weapon Infusion Aura  |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
| Sharp Rune        | +15% Base Physical | Metallic Silver Edge Gleam   |
| Flame Rune        | +20 Fire / Burn DoT| Flaming Orange Particle Wash |
| Frost Rune        | +25% Slow Duration | Cyan Crystalline Shimmer     |
| Volt Rune         | +20% Shock Chain   | Arc Lightning Crackle        |
| Echo Rune         | +30% Armor Pierce  | Concentric Soundwave Rings   |
| Void Rune         | +10% Lifesteal     | Dark Purple Nebula Trail     |
+-------------------+--------------------+------------------------------+
```

### Dynamic Compound Name Generation:
The game dynamically alters the weapon's name, lore, and visual sprite tint based on slotted runes:
- Base: `Iron Sword` ($22\text{ DMG}$)
- Socket 1 (`Sharp`): $\to$ `Sharp Iron Sword` ($25.3\text{ DMG}$)
- Socket 2 (`Flame`): $\to$ `Sharp Flame Iron Sword` ($25.3\text{ DMG} + \text{Burn}$)
- Socket 3 (`Echo`): $\to$ `Sharp Flame Echo Iron Sword` ($25.3\text{ DMG} + \text{Burn} + \text{Armor Pierce}$)

When fully socketed, your weapon radiates an immaculate particle aura. Bosses will literally rethink their life choices.

## 🛡️ 4. The 6 Equipment Slots

Press `[E]` to open the unified **Inventory & Equipment Window**. Your build is shaped by 6 modular armor and accessory slots:

1. **Head (Goggles / Helms)**: Grants tactical vision buffs (e.g. *Explorer Goggles* highlight ores through stone walls).
2. **Body (Jackets / Plate Armor)**: Primary physical defense and maximum health boost.
3. **Gloves (Gauntlets / Vambraces)**: Attack speed, mining swing speed, and block placement reach.
4. **Boots (Runner Boots / Greaves)**: Movement speed, jump velocity, and fall damage mitigation.
5. **Charm (Amulets / Relics)**: Elemental resistance and Spark Energy regeneration rates.
6. **Core (The Heartbeat Resonance)**: The crown jewel slot. Equipping an Echo Core grants passive transformation perks (e.g. *Ember Core* makes you immune to lava).
