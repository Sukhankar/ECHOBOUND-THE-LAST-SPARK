## ⚡ Welcome to the Official EchoBound Wiki!

Listen up chat, let us cook for a minute. You just woke up in **Aethelgard**, an open-world voxel sandbox realm that was vibing in 4K surround sound until one fateful night when the global audio jack got yanked out of the socket. Every single **Resonance Tower** went dead silent. The machines lost their minds, the wildlife developed severe trust issues, and **The Conductor** — basically the Fleet Admiral of the Static Hierarchy — decided that total silence is the only acceptable law. 

Bro really thought he could mute the universe and nobody would push back. **Major L.**

Enter **Rin**, our cyan-scarf-donning protagonist with astronomical Main Character Energy (Gear 5 Luffy level reckless optimism), accompanied by **Pip**, a floating ball of pure ancient frequency who gives major Tony Tony Chopper emergency mascot energy. Together with an eccentric crew of survivors, you're setting sail across treacherous biomes to recover the **Echo Cores** (the Poneglyphs of Sound) and ignite **The Last Spark** (The One Piece) before the world is locked in permanent buffering.

## 🚀 Quick Start: Pro Gamer Cheat Sheet

- **Controls**: `WASD` to move, `Space` to jump/glide, `Shift` to sprint/dash, `Left-Click` to attack/mine, `Right-Click` to place block/interact.
- **Inventory & Gear**: Press `[E]` to pop the 6-slot equipment & runecrafting bag.
- **Crafting & Recipes**: Press `[C]` anywhere or stand near a Workbench/Forge for advanced tiers.
- **Quest Log & Lore**: Press `[Q]` to track the 4-tier chronicles and main objectives.
- **Emergency Help & Tutorial**: Press `[H]` or `[F1]` at any time for the interactive controls manual.
